title: 4️⃣ Built with privacy in mind
section: features
---

All operations are 100% local. Aktivisda runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to a server, not even your images or texts. Compression is done with WebAssembly (oxipng, mozjpeg), directly on your device.

In a few months, you will be able to blur faces, hide sensitive areas, and remove metadata. No tracking. No leaks. Full control.
